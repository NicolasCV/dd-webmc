# family: prop

One plate per `onExamine`. Examining is half the player's verbs and it currently renders as prose
alone. These are marginalia — they ship small (~180px on the page) and `loading="lazy"`, so nothing
downloads until the player looks at something.

Specimen framing, which is also the framing that survives `multiply` best: an object alone on bare
paper with nothing behind it.

## _defaults

`4:3` · `768` · `refs: styleboard, paper, anchor`

## _framing

```
FRAMING: a single object isolated on bare paper, 4:3 landscape. Specimen plate
from a field guide. No room, no scene, no ground line, no cast shadow beyond a
light hatched one. The object floats on the paper and its hatching dissolves
before the edge.
```

## prop-scribe

```
SUBJECT: a standing corpse in the dress of a clerk — long coat, ink-horn on a
```

that shows it: the same dead clerk, moved by force or moved by argument.

```
Keep this exact figure, drawing and lighting. Change only its attitude: it has
stepped a pace to one side and turned slightly away, and the tally-stick is now
```
