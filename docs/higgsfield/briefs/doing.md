# family: doing

The companion at work. One plate per (character × thing only that character can do), which makes
this family a picture of the README's **asymmetric capability** section: Brakka is the only one who
can shoulder the door, Ilke is the only one who can pick the vault, Wen is the only one who can read
the murals. Nobody is being polite — the tools genuinely aren't there, and neither are the plates.

Half-figure, not a bust: these show hands and the thing being done.

## _defaults

`3:2` · `1200` · `refs: styleboard, paper, anchor`

## _framing

```
FRAMING: half-figure action vignette, 3:2 landscape. The person is shown from
roughly the waist up, engaged with an object, and the object is as much the
subject as they are. Hands visible and doing the work. The scene dissolves into
bare paper on all sides — no room, no floor, no walls beyond what is touched.
Light from the upper left.
```

## doing-brakka-force_door

`tier 2` · `3:2` · `1200` · `→ public/art/doing-brakka-force_door.webp` · `refs: char-brakka`

```
SUBJECT: the heavyset scarred man in the mail coif and brass-clasped gorget,
driving his shoulder into a great iron-banded door. Caught at the moment of
impact — body low and turned, both boots braced, one forearm flat against the
timber. The head is down and turned away from us so the face is barely read. Dust
jumping off the iron band. Only the section of door he is hitting is drawn.
```

## doing-brakka-intimidate_scribe

`tier 2` · `3:2` · `1200` · `→ public/art/doing-brakka-intimidate_scribe.webp` · `refs: char-brakka`

```
SUBJECT: the heavyset scarred man leaning in toward a thin upright figure in a
clerk's long coat, close enough to be a problem. One of his hands is planted flat
somewhere out of frame, taking his weight. He is talking, mouth open on a short
word. The clerk is drawn lighter and does not react at all — no expression, eyes
open, perfectly still. The contrast between them is the plate.
```

## doing-wen-examine_bones

```
SUBJECT: the older woman crouched beside a seated skeleton slumped against the
```

## doing-wen-persuade_scribe

`tier 2` · `3:2` · `1200` · `→ public/art/doing-wen-persuade_scribe.webp` · `refs: char-wen`

```
SUBJECT: the older woman standing level with the thin clerk-corpse, addressing it
directly and without drama, as one clerk to another. Both are upright, at the
same height, at a polite distance. Her hands are open and low. The corpse holds a
long notched tally-stick at its side. She is being reasonable at a dead thing and
it is going rather well.
```

## doing-ilke-pick_vault

`tier 2` · `3:2` · `1200` · `→ public/art/doing-ilke-pick_vault.webp` · `refs: char-ilke`

```
SUBJECT: the lean hooded figure crouched at a heavy vault door, working the lock.
Both hands at the keyhole, two slender picks held in a practised grip, wrists
turned. The head is tipped and turned so the ear is toward the lock rather than
the eye — listening, not looking. Face mostly lost inside the hood. Only the lock
plate and a hand's width of door are drawn.
```
